Who holds the money, in plain words

Escrow only protects you if you know how it works. This is how it works here.

Held for your order only

Your payment is held in escrow by Houlio Duo for that one order. It goes to the seller when you confirm delivery, or back to you if a dispute is decided in your favour.

USDT and USDC escrow runs on audited contracts, so every lock, release and refund is recorded on-chain.

Every order shows one of these states

  1. Awaiting payment
  2. Payment detected
  3. Payment confirmed
  4. Funds secured
  5. Seller notified
  6. Order processing
  7. Shipped or delivered
  8. Awaiting buyer confirmation
  9. Completed
  10. Released to seller
  11. If something goes wrong
  12. Refund requested
  13. Disputed
  14. Under review
  15. Refunded
